Topic

In their dimly lit hideout, Tomatina, Blake, and Elle Gorithm gather around a wooden table to review the museum blueprints. Standing at the high-security jewel museum vault, Tomatina uses her tools to crack open the heavy vault door. Inside the vault room, Elle Gorithm reaches out and carefully lifts the glowing diamond out of its glass display case. Suddenly, a security guard enters from the far doorway, raising his flashlight and shouting to freeze. Tomatina quickly hurls a fresh red tomato at the security guard's face to create a messy distraction. While Tomatina slips away into the shadows, the security guard wipes his eyes and snaps steel handcuffs onto the wrists of Blake and Elle Gorithm.
